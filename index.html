💡 Fine-Tuning Quick Tips
Eye Fatigue / Motion Sickness: Lower the Sensitivity and increase the Smoothing Filter to 15-20%.
Input Lag / Delayed Camera: Reduce the Smoothing Filter (below 10%) for a sharper, immediate camera response.
Straight-line Wobble: Set the Deadzone to 1° or 2° to filter out minor wheel micro-adjustments.
