Main Hub & Presets Setup Guide & FAQ

🛠️ Setting up LookAhead Steering Controller

  1. Download & Extract: Download the specific version for your simulator (RBR, ACRally, or AC Evo) from the main page and extract the ZIP archive.
  2. OpenTrack Bridge Setup: Install and open OpenTrack. Set the Input protocol to UDP over network and verify port 4242 is open.
  3. Launch LookAhead: Run LookAhead Steering Controller.exe as Administrator before launching your simulator.
  4. Load Preset: Select a recommended preset from our main hub matching your current driving discipline.

📐 Setting up Pro-Sim FOV Utility

  1. Measure Your Setup: Measure your screen size (diagonal in inches), viewing distance from eye level to screen (cm), and monitor bezel thickness.
  2. Input Data: Enter these values into Pro-Sim FOV Utility to calculate your exact mathematical field of view.
  3. Apply Config: Copy the calculated horizontal or vertical FOV directly into your simulator's graphics/camera settings.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does LookAhead require a webcam or head-tracker?
No hardware is required! LookAhead translates your steering wheel input directly into dynamic camera rotation.
Will LookAhead cause input lag?
The utility operates via high-frequency UDP packets at 60Hz. If you notice any camera delay, simply lower the Smoothing Filter setting to below 10%.
Is Pro-Sim FOV Utility suitable for Triple Monitor setups?
Yes, it fully supports Single, Ultrawide, and Triple Monitor angle/bezel calculations.
